About

I am a Project Associate-I with Prof. M. S. Madhusudhan at IISER Pune, where I completed a BS-MS dual degree with a major in Physics (2020–2025). My master’s thesis was an industrial research internship at BIOVIA, Dassault Systèmes, Cambridge, UK, on applications of multi-scale simulations to battery material modeling.

Research Experience

Project Associate-I

June 2026 – Present

With Prof. M. S. Madhusudhan, IISER Pune

  • Partial charges: Computing DFT-derived partial charges for small proteins including explicit water with allosteric sites in different environments to improve electrostatics in MD; with training ML models to extend to large datasets.
  • D-peptides: Designing D-peptides (protease-resistant peptidomimetics) to bind naturally occurring L-protein targets, starting from small therapeutic peptides.
  • Ab–Ag: Protein–protein docking (HADDOCK3) and MD (GROMACS, CHARMM36m) combined with ML to compare how wild-type and mutant antibodies bind their antigen.
  • Clinical trial: Developing a biomarker to find responders of a phase 1 trial oncology drug by analysing longitudinal immune cell data for experimental industry collaborators at Zumutor Biologics.

Research Assistant

June 2025 – May 2026

With Prof. Arnab Mukherjee, IISER Pune

  • AlphaToBeta: Built a deep reinforcement learning framework for protein sequence design in PyTorch. It proposes amino-acid mutations that drive α-helix → β-sheet transitions, using ESM protein-language-model embeddings and ESMFold. Trained with PPO using the Open-AI Gymnasium RL library and extended the reward with the residue composition within 6 Å of the mutated segment to learn which local environments favour β-sheet formation.
  • ML collective variables: Developed machine-learned collective variables for RNA folding (TICA, LDA, autoencoders via mlcolvar), with water coordinates as inputs to CVs in enhanced-sampling free-energy calculations (PLUMED, GROMACS).

Tools: RDKit, VAE, ESM, ESMFold, stable-baselines3, PLUMED, mlcolvar, TICA, LDA, autoencoders

Master’s Thesis / Industrial Research Internship

May 2024 – May 2025

Supervisor: Dr. Struan Robertson, Materials Studio, BIOVIA, Dassault Systèmes, Cambridge, UK · Thesis report

  • Built a comprehensive multiscale modelling framework for Li-ion cathode manufacturing that links granular dynamics (DEM), atomistic and mesoscale MD (LAMMPS, Materials Studio), and ML-based force-field parameterization.
  • Implemented methods from the literature in C++ (100 times faster than previous work), and wrote Python, Perl, and Fiji pipelines to compute porosity and tortuosity from images.
  • Ran sensitivity analyses on experimentally hard-to-determine to guide predictive material and process design.
  • Benchmarked performance of Materials Studio against LAMMPS and found parameters that led to 2.5 times speed up in simulations.
  • Methods and workflows developed in this project were integrated into BIOVIA Materials Studio (2025 Release) Granular Dynamics package, making them available to industrial users.
